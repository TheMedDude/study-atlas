# Change record template

Use for each feature/content change; append concise results to PROJECT_LOG.md and supersede relevant decisions. Facts should be understandable without the chat.

- **Problem and trigger:** what a student/author encounters, with a concrete before example.
- **Requested outcome and scope:** confirmed requirements; distinguish proposals.
- **Decision:** chosen behavior/structure, reason and alternatives only where they explain a real tradeoff.
- **Affected contracts:** source authority, IDs/revisions, storage, backup, accessibility, routes and module responsibilities.
- **Implementation:** changed files/components and resulting behavior.
- **Content review:** author/reviewer, source pages, uncertainty, repairs and acceptance; not applicable for UI-only work.
- **Verification:** exact commands, tests, rendered sizes/inputs, artifact/commit identifiers, and limitations.
- **Release:** repository commit, CI and deployment separately; live checks and rollback target.
- **Remaining work:** honest bugs/debt and next acceptance gate, without claiming proposed features are complete.
