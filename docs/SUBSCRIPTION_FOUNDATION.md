# Future subscription platform: foundation, not shipped scope

The owner intends to turn these small files into a subscription site, possibly continued with Claude. This records reusable assets and open decisions. It does not authorize billing or promise a launch date.

## Reuse

Source hashes/manifests, faithful extraction, uncertainty/coverage, stable reviewed activity IDs/revisions and option evidence, bilingual explanations, author/reviewer workflow, pure scoring, subject/scope separation, backup compatibility, design tokens/responsive contracts, tests and release evidence.

## Introduce distinct boundaries

1. **Authoring:** draft -> source QA -> candidate -> independent review -> approved -> published -> superseded/withdrawn. Keep author/reviewer events and evidence, not just validated booleans.
2. **Delivery:** versioned catalogs/releases and server-enforced entitlements. Published content revisions remain immutable. Control media delivery where required.
3. **Student data:** authenticated private attempts/bookmarks/session state, explicit local/cloud save states, conflicts, export/deletion and backup. Coverage stays separate from performance.
4. **Commercial:** plans, subscriptions, cancellation, receipts and verified webhooks. Trusted server enforcement; no secrets in client and no hidden-button paywall.
5. **Operations:** review/audit logs, rollback, accessibility/performance budgets, monitoring/support.

Do not upload current device histories as fixtures. Design explicit migration/import consent before cloud sync. Legacy backups need a documented migration path.

## Decide before selecting a stack

Who pays and for which courses/periods? Who holds commercial rights to files, diagrams and author contributions? Which markets/currencies/ages? Semester, subject or catalog access? Free previews? After cancellation? Retention/deletion/export? Who can author, approve, publish and correct?

These remain open. Owner permission for public sharing does not establish institutions'/lecturers' commercial licensing. Resolve rights and market before committing to paid content delivery.

## Migration sequence

Stabilize/document current experience -> validate content workflow -> agree commercial/rights requirements -> design service boundaries/API contracts -> select hosting/auth/payment -> small authenticated pilot -> verify security, billing, privacy and content -> gradual launch.

A framework rewrite isn't prerequisite for better questions/phone layouts. Extract the large controller incrementally, keeping tests/contracts. Avoid rewriting scoring, persistence and design simultaneously.

## Truthful limits

Current app has no account service, paid entitlements, encrypted cloud progress, billing, guaranteed offline mode or calibrated mastery model. Schema tests do not certify science or commercial rights. Release claims must match implemented/verified behavior.
