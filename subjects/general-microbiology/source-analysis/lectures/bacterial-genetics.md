## PDF page 36 — Homologous and nonhomologous recombination

> **Source:** [Original PDF, page 36](../../sources/slides/02-bacterial-genetics.pdf#page=36). **Printed slide:** not shown. **Classification:** teaching.

After DNA transfers **from donor to recipient**, it can integrate into the **host-cell chromosome by recombination**.

- **Homologous recombination:** two DNA pieces with **extensive homologous regions** pair and exchange pieces through **breakage and reunion**.
- **Nonhomologous recombination:** **little, if any, homology** is necessary.

Different **genetic loci** govern the types and different **enzymes** are involved. **Endonucleases and ligases** are named, without an enzyme-by-type assignment.
