## PDF page 6 - Polyadenylation


> **Source:** [Original PDF, page 6](../../sources/slides/05-rna-metabolism-2.pdf#page=6). **Printed slide:** not shown. **Classification:** teaching.


- The **poly A tail** contains **80-250 A residues**.
- It protects RNA from enzymatic degradation.
- Enzyme: **Polyadenylate polymerase**.
- Reaction: **RNA + nATP → RNA-(AMP)n + nPPi**, with **n = 80-250**.
- Requires **cleaved RNA as a primer**; **no template** is needed.

The figure shows RNA polymerase on template DNA and a capped RNA containing the cleavage signal **AAUAAA**. An **enzyme complex** associates with this sequence. An **endonuclease** cuts **10-30 nucleotides 3′ to the sequence**, leaving an **OH(3′)** on the retained RNA. **Polyadenylate polymerase** then uses ATP, releases PPi, and produces **AAA(A)n-OH(3′)**.


**Visual reconstruction / check:** All figure labels and reaction direction checked; distinct endonuclease cleavage and polymerase addition stages retained. Do not treat all processing events as strictly sequential beyond this displayed reaction.
