## How the facts behave
Cell injury repeatedly links a cause to a cellular target, altered process, morphology and fate. ATP depletion, calcium, reactive oxygen species and membrane injury recur across parts, while the adaptive responses and death patterns are easily confused. Source-supported exceptions matter: tissue and setting determine how a mechanism appears. Classifications include adaptation, necrosis, accumulations and calcification. Exact numerical windows occur less often and are assessed only with their stated conditions.

Inflammation repeatedly connects mediators to cellular or vascular events, recruitment steps and outcomes. Selectins, integrins and PECAM-1 must remain distinct; chemotaxis, phagocytosis and killing are related processes, not synonyms. Acute/chronic inflammation, granulation tissue/granuloma and macrophage activation patterns invite comparisons. Healing outcomes depend on the persistence of injury, tissue destruction and regenerative capacity. An explicit taught process supports ordering, but a mediator list does not. Counts follow meaningful coverage. Dense pages can support multiple genuinely different distinctions; repeated diagrams support the existing target rather than duplicate questions.

The uncertainty register preserves disputed nomenclature, chemical classifications, causal wording and broad table shorthand. Those claims cannot determine a score. Clear claims on the same page can still be tested if the uncertain claim is not needed to distinguish any option.
