## PDF page 17 — Reactions of the Citric Acid Cycle

Kind: teaching. Printed slide: not visible. Visual image inspected.

[Original page image](../../../../work/metabolic-biochemistry/extraction/citric-acid-cycle-and-pdh/page-017.png)

### Faithful slide text

```text
                                                                                                               Dr. Osama Essawi 2025
           Reactions of the
            Citric Acid Cycle


⬣   In each cycle turn, one acetyl group enters
    as acetyl-CoA, and two molecules of CO2
    are released.

⬣   One molecule of oxaloacetate is used to
    form citrate, and one is regenerated.


⬣   Four of the eight steps involve oxidations,
    conserving energy in the form of NADH
    and FADH2.

⬣   NADH and FADH2 donate electrons to the
    respiratory chain, driving ATP synthesis.
```

### Figure, image and layout transcription

Full labeled cycle: 1 aldol condensation; 2a dehydration and 2b rehydration reposition citrate OH in isocitrate to set up next decarboxylation; 3 oxidative decarboxylation, OH oxidized to carbonyl and CO2 lost; 4 oxidative decarboxylation with PDH-like mechanism dependent on carbonyl on adjacent carbon; 5 substrate-level phosphorylation, thioester energy conserved in GTP/ATP phosphoanhydride; 6 dehydrogenation introduces double bond and initiates methylene oxidation sequence; 7 hydration introduces OH for next oxidation; 8 OH dehydrogenation gives carbonyl positioned for aldol condensation. NADH at isocitrate, α-ketoglutarate and malate steps; FADH2 at succinate step; GTP/ATP at succinyl-CoA step; two CO2 at steps3 and4; CoA-SH at step1 and5; CoA-SH entry at step4.

### Indexed relationships

- **Cycle entry, CO2 and oxaloacetate regeneration:** Each turn takes one acetyl group as acetyl-CoA, releases two CO2, uses one oxaloacetate to make citrate and regenerates one oxaloacetate.
- **Four oxidation steps conserve energy:** Four of eight steps oxidize substrates and conserve energy in NADH/FADH2; those carriers donate to respiratory chain and drive ATP synthesis.
- **Citric acid cycle sequence and enzymes:** Citrate synthase: OAA+acetyl-CoA→citrate; aconitase: citrate↔cis-aconitate↔isocitrate; isocitrate dehydrogenase→α-ketoglutarate; α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase→succinyl-CoA; succinyl-CoA synthetase→succinate; succinate dehydrogenase→fumarate; fumarase→malate; malate dehydrogenase→OAA.
