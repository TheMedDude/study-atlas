## PDF page 19 — Regulation by cellular energy needs

Classification: teaching. Printed slide: not separately numbered. [Original page image](../../sources/pages/oxidative-phosphorylation/page-019.webp).

### Faithful text extraction

Dr. Osama Essawi 2025
     Oxidative Phosphorylation Is Regulated by Cellular
                                                Energy Needs



⬣    The rate of O₂ consumption in mitochondria is regulated by ADP availability (acceptor control of respiration).

⬣    Cellular energy status is indicated by intracellular ADP concentration and the           [ATP]/[ADP]·[Pi ]  “mass-action
     ratio” (which is typically high  ).

⬣    When energy demand increases (e.g., during protein synthesis), ATP is broken down to ADP + Pi, lowering the
     mass-action ratio.

⬣    Increased ADP availability stimulates oxidative phosphorylation, leading to higher respiration rates and ATP
     regeneration.

⬣    This cycle ensures a stable ATP supply, matching ATP formation to consumption, maintaining cellular energy
     balance.
