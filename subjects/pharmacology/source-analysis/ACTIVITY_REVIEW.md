# Independent activity review

603 submitted candidates were reviewed chapter by chapter; 65 duplicate, weak or underspecified items were removed. The accepted bank has 538 activities: 483 five-option MCQs, 51 matching and 4 ordering.

Introduction: root author, separate ANS reviewer; 66 accepted. Pharmacokinetics: PK author, separate PD reviewer; 180 accepted. Pharmacodynamics: PD author, separate ANS reviewer; 99 accepted. ANS/Adrenergic: ANS author, separate PK reviewer; 193 accepted. Each accepted record carries per-option evidence, bilingual explanation, exact source references and an independent reviewer record.

Review corrected second-supported-answer ambiguities, overlapping distractors, non-unique ordering, implicit source framing, incomplete calculation conditions, Arabic wording and exact page alignment. A separate cue audit flagged length/format/universal-word patterns and all remaining flags were repaired before acceptance. Structural checks supplement this record-by-record review.

483 assessable core concepts have activities; 33 other core-index entries have justified deferrals. There are 76 explicit deferrals overall; other supporting examples remain indexed without activities. No unresolved claim is used to determine a scored answer. The first three chapters prioritize principles; drug-specific comparisons belong in chapter four.
