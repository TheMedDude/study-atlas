## PDF page 28 — Tolerance versus tachyphylaxis

> **Source:** [Original PDF, page 28](../../sources/slides/03-pharmacodynamics.pdf#page=28). Printed slide: 28. Classification: teaching.

Tolerance vs Tachyphylaxis
•Tolerance: Tolerance is the gradual adaptation of the body to a drug,
resulting in the need for increased doses to achieve the same
therapeutic effect that was initially obtained with a lower dose.
Example, Morphine tolerance.
•Tachyphylaxis: This refers to a rapid decrease in the effectiveness of a
drug after its initial administration. It can occur after just a few doses
and is characterized by a quick onset of diminished response, often
within a short time frame.
