## PDF page 17 — NE synthesis-storage-release-removal

> Source: [Original PDF](../../sources/slides/04-ans-and-adrenergic-drugs.pdf#page=17). Printed slide: not shown. Scope: teaching.

### Visual reconstruction / table and diagram detail

1.Synthesis of NE: tyrosine hydroxylation is rate-limiting; Na+ accompanies tyrosine uptake; tyrosine -> DOPA -> dopamine.
2.Uptake into storage vesicles: dopamine enters and becomes NE; vesicular NE protected from degradation; reserpine inhibits transport into vesicle.
3.Release: calcium influx triggers vesicle/cell-membrane fusion(exocytosis); guanethidine and bretylium block release.
4.Binding: neurotransmitter activates postsynaptic receptor; presynaptic receptor also drawn.
5.Removal: released NE rapidly taken into neuron; cocaine/imipramine inhibit reuptake.
6.Metabolism: COMT methylates NE; MAO oxidizes it. Inactive metabolites -> urine. Other labels: synaptic space, intracellular response, cell membrane, COMT.
