# Study Atlas

Study Atlas helps students study their own course materials through reviewed questions, connected reference tools and exact source-page access. Its current users are the owner and friends; the owner intends to build a subscription product later.

## Product commitments

- Source fidelity precedes question volume. No outside teaching facts silently repair an uncertain slide.
- Questions challenge distinctions and applications, with plausible alternatives rather than answer cues.
- English questions and English/Arabic explanations retain English technical terms where useful.
- No typed answers, self-grading or confidence prompts. Browsing and revealing facts do not create scored evidence.
- Course coverage, answer performance and insufficient evidence are distinct. Recognition is not proof of unaided recall.
- Phone and iPad studying are primary use cases, alongside desktop. A small screen must not inherit an unusable desktop layout.
- Organization should be simple and obvious. The user permits visual improvements; retain current recognizable assets unless an intentional new direction is agreed.
- Current progress is device-local with backup/restore. No student account, billing, synchronization or complete offline guarantee exists.

## Current implementation

A dependency-free static browser app with three source-backed subjects: Molecular Biology, General Microbiology and Pharmacology. Question banks, scope, source files, settings, answer history and study state are separate. See docs/START_HERE.md for verified counts, contracts and continuation.

## Future business direction

Subscriptions are an ambition, not shipped behavior. The next platform needs decisions about audience, licensed content, accounts, entitlements, payment, privacy, author/reviewer roles and operations. See docs/SUBSCRIPTION_FOUNDATION.md. Do not implement a paywall or promise cloud saving as part of the current design release.
